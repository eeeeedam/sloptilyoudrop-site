About the project

Get in loser, we're going slopping.

In a slop-saturated world, meaning, information, and values are transforming before our eyes. What started as irreverent, goofy, AI-generated content has seeped into everyday life: wedding invitations, restaurant menus, holiday cards, and the far more meaningful places we once reserved for human hands.

It’s easy to find frustration and anger at AI. It's harder to quantify or diagnose what exactly is being lost.

This project tracks the sociocultural cost of AI slop. As AI is granted authorship in spaces that used to be human, new questions swirl: What’s acceptable once AI artifacts are indistinguishable from human work? What is the cost of using tools that mimic humanity but lack its randomness, patina, and imperfections? What’s eroding?

The Slop Galaxy is a living map of the moments AI-generated content crossed a line and changed our norms.

So grab your popcorn, your pen, and your slop-hunting boots. View the Slop Galaxy to see what’s been tracked to date or submit some slop in the form below.

How to read it

Every node is a documented moment. Here’s the logic behind what you’re looking at.

01
Size is impact

The larger the node, the more consequential the moment. Scored 1–10, it’s a curated judgment of how much this event moved the world.

02
Slop vs. Spill

The vertical axis is the argument. Higher nodes are slop — the artifacts: fake images, ghost-written articles, synthetic personas. Lower nodes are spills — the consequences: lawsuits, bans, legislation, cultural reckonings. The same moment can be both. The axis shows where it landed.

03
The through-line

Two arcs connect the defining moments — one tracing the slop, one tracing the spills. Each segment is annotated. The gap between them is the argument. Toggle it on from the sidebar. Follow both. That’s the thesis.

04
Every node has a story

Tap any node to read what happened, why it matters, and where to go deeper. Select nodes carry an editor’s note.
